What we build

Full vertical control · Open-source full stack


Redundant Mechatronic Lab designs and builds industrial machines with full vertical control — mechanics, electronics, firmware, and the software that drives them, all in-house.

Most of our work is open source; the rest is shared through licensed collaboration. We welcome partnerships, contributions, and discussions on technology development and optimization.

The aerostatic spindle is the one project we didn't build alone — grant-funded, developed with a research institute. We are adapting the technology and plan to open-source it in the coming years. If you want to work with us, get in touch.
